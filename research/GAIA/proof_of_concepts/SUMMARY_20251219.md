## Key Insight

**PAC Hierarchy = Inductive Bias**

The hierarchy encodes assumptions:
- Tokens belong to categories
- Categories belong to supercategories
- Higher levels generalize to lower levels

This is a **learned prior** encoded in tree structure - the same thing neural networks learn through millions of gradient updates, but represented explicitly.
