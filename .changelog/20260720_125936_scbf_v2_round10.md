## Findings (journal: journals/2026-07-20_round10_verdict.md)

1. **Cloze probe (generation-free): round-9 deflation excluded — knowledge erosion
   confirmed at full strength** (experts arm ΔLL −0.230, 10× the 2×SE threshold;
   top-1 −6.6pt). Bonus: **full arm significantly POSITIVE** (ΔLL +0.060, top-1
   +2.0) — distributed continuous wiki-reading ADDED fact knowledge.
2. **Mechanism hypothesis REFUTED — the null is the discovery**: both arms put
   statistically identical update mass on the same experts (ratio 0.987, corr
   0.981; mass tracks usage ρ=0.756, no amplification). The protective difference
   is the ~5% trunk co-adapting. **Refined law: uncompensated updates damage;
   compensated updates don't.** Round 9's lesion localized the vulnerability
   (storage-without-readout-freedom), not storage per se — the classic
   neuroscience lesion subtlety, reproduced by the gradient instrument.
3. Continuous-learning verdict at the 5k dose: distributed adaptation = stream
   gains + fact gains + 9/9 retention; concentrated adaptation = silent knowledge
   destruction invisible to CE.
