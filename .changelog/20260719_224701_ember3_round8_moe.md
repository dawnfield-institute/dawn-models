## Findings (journal: journals/2026-07-20_round8_moe.md)

1. **Works**: domain gains −0.14..−0.18 CE vs off-domain forgetting only +0.03..+0.04
   after 5k updates — the pretrained MoE keeps its learnings while learning.
2. **Trunk-freeze intuition INVERTED at this dose**: full-model adaptation beats
   experts-only on BOTH stream edge (+0.179 vs +0.135) and off-domain retention
   (+0.026 vs +0.043 forgetting). Hypothesis: concentrating updates concentrates the
   damage. Long-horizon crossover (trunk-pristine property) = the follow-up.
3. Router-skew cross-validation with Colibrì (42.7% base vs 47.6% instruct top-26%).
4. Instrument bug logged: stacked expert tensors nulled per-expert attribution
   (fix: row-norms in hook); expert-localization question deferred one round.
