## Findings

1. **Ablation**: gravity (entropy-baseline pullback) is the standout CIMM mechanism —
   halves drift with qclip, best held-ahead MAE; CIMM's every-10 gate costs early
   learning and its rollback validator rejects a suspicious ~50%. Pre-stated winner
   rule selected the plain repaired substrate for the origin arms.
2. **Gate-origin arms (headline)**: per-token update gating at matched rate separates
   by signal origin — **exogenous (realized error) > random > endogenous (entropy)**,
   non-overlapping seed ranges on late competence edge AND plasticity. The endogenous
   gate drifts least while performing worst = the pre-defined ossification failure
   mode, caught by the paired-metric design. First positive empirical support for the
   Ember III exogeneity thesis, at the update-gating level round 1 localized.
